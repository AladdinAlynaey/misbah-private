<p align="center"><a href="#arabic">العربية</a> · <b>English</b></p>

<img src=".github/assets/hero-en.jpg" alt="Misbah: an Arabic-first platform for agents, automation and apps. Build your agent, automate your work, design your team’s tools." width="100%">

<br>

Misbah brings together, in one being, what other platforms keep apart: intelligent agents that think and converse, automation that executes precisely and waits for your approval on WhatsApp, apps your team works in, and a genie that builds all of it from a wish written in Arabic. Every world stands on one foundation of connections, knowledge, channels and approvals, so nothing is built twice.

<br>
